Basalt Infrastructure Vulnerability Disclosure Program

Program last updated 2025-11-03

In scope: Basalt Hypervisor, Basalt Storage Array and basalt.example web properties.

Basalt Infrastructure authorizes good-faith security testing of in-scope assets.

Submit findings through this platform’s report form.

Basalt Infrastructure will not take legal action against researchers who follow this policy.

Reporters may publish their findings 90 days after submission or once a fix is released.

What to include

A good report describes the issue, the affected product and version, and the steps needed to reproduce it.

We aim to acknowledge each report within five business days and to keep the reporter informed of our progress until the issue is resolved.
