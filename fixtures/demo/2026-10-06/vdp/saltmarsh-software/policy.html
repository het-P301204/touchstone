Vulnerability Disclosure Policy

Last updated: May 2, 2023

Scope

This policy applies to all Saltmarsh Software products, services and web properties, including:

  • Saltmarsh ERP
  • Tidewater Portal

Authorization

We authorize security testing by members of the public of any system or product in scope, provided you follow this policy.

Reporting

Report vulnerabilities by email to security@saltmarsh.example or through our web form at https://saltmarsh.example/security/report.

Please include the affected product, version and steps to reproduce.

Safe harbor

If you make a good faith effort to comply with this policy during your security research, we will not recommend or pursue legal action related to your research.

Disclosure

We support coordinated disclosure. You may publicly disclose your findings after we release a fix or 90 days after your report, whichever comes first.

Thank you for helping keep our customers safe — we’re grateful.
